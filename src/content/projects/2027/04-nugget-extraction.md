title: Nugget extraction
stage: screening
areas: [3, 4]
question: Can we extract the value-bearing pieces of information a systematic review depends on, and build and evaluate a review from them?
---

Starting from a collection of relevant documents produced by earlier stages (retrieval, ranking, classification), extract unit-level pieces of information, called nuggets, that are highly relevant or even crucial for writing the systematic review. Unlike in previous work, the nuggets are value-based and cannot be found by simple lexical matching. "Aspirin reduces recurrent strokes" is a different nugget from the same claim reported with its effect size and 95% confidence interval.

The project includes evaluating the extracted nuggets with LLM judges, rubrics from the systematic-review literature and automated measures. It also includes combining the nuggets to generate a full review, and evaluating that review.
