question: Do agents stop searching once they have found evidence for the answer they expect?
background: LLM agents, experimental design

**In eight weeks.** Give agents an expected answer, in the protocol or the prompt, and measure how their queries, screening decisions and stopping points shift compared with a neutral run.

**As a thesis.** Mitigations, such as an explicit search for disconfirming evidence, and whether they change the conclusions agents reach.

**Starting data.** Reviews with a clear pooled effect; the baseline agent from [An open action log and a baseline agent](#an-open-action-log-and-a-baseline-agent).
