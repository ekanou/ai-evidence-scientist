**In eight weeks.** Build pooled, query-independent reference sets from the union of system runs and the studies the published review included. Measure how system rankings change with pool depth and with which systems contribute to the pool.

**As a thesis.** Estimate how many relevant studies no system found, and how much uncertainty that adds to every recall figure.

**Starting data.** Runs from [From protocol to search strategy](#from-protocol-to-search-strategy), or from existing technology-assisted review collections.
