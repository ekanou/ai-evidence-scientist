title: Outcome-aware evaluation
---

The cost of a missed study depends on what else was found. We will score systems by the stability of their conclusions, building on work that re-ran meta-analyses under simulated screening ([Norman et al., 2019](#ref-norman-2019)) and re-scored systems by review outcomes ([Kusa et al., 2023](#ref-kusa-2023)). Any LLM-based judge will be validated against expert methodologists; correlation with past system rankings is not enough.
