title: The decision to search again
---

Agent confidence is a poor stopping signal: model uncertainty collapses once retrieved text is in context, whether or not that text is relevant ([Soudani et al., 2025](#ref-soudani-2025)). We will study stopping rules that are calibrated and accountable, and the two failure modes that matter most: searching for confirmation and stopping too early.
